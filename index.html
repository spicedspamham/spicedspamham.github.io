SHRINKAGE

An MTG Variant by Joseph DeSantis

Quick Rules

  • 50-card singleton decks — deplete your opponent's library to win
  • Max hand size 5, draw 2 per turn, discard at turn start
  • Roll a d6 + 6 base = up to 12 resource points; save up to 6
  • All creatures have Deathtouch — excess damage mills opponent
  • Mill 5 cards at the end of each of your turns
  • No colors — use converted mana cost as resource cost
