Rules

Deck = health.
When a deck reaches 0, that player loses.

Turn flow:
1 Draw card
2 Roll resources (6 + d6)
3 Play soldiers
4 End turn → combat → enemy turn
Unblocked attacks shrink enemy deck equal to strength.
(Enemy attacks back the same way)
