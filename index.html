Rules

Goal: force opponents to run out of cards in their deck. When your deck reaches 0 you lose.

Deck size: 50 cards

Limits:
⭐ 1-star → max 5 copies
⭐⭐ 2-star → max 3 copies
⭐⭐⭐ 3-star → max 1 copy

Max star counts in deck:
⭐⭐⭐ = 5 cards
⭐⭐ = 15 cards
⭐ = remainder

Turn Phases
1 Draw (draw 2)
2 Resource (6 + d6)
3 Main
4 Combat

Combat: When soldiers clash both die. Excess damage shrinks opponent's deck.

Shrink = move cards from deck to Spoils.
