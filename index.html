Protocol Rules

  • • SHRINK: Your deck is your life. Take damage, mill cards.
  • • HASTE: Every soldier can attack the moment it lands.
  • • BLOCK: Defenders must intercept attackers. Both die in clash.
  • • ENERGY: Capped at 6. Refills every turn.

Symbolic Logic

$X: Shrink Enemy X
#X: Shrink Self X
@X: Add Energy X
[S+]: Sustenance
%: Counter Stack
^: Recall Grave

How to Play

Choose your mode (AI or P2P). Click cards in hand to put them on the Stack. You must click Pass Priority to resolve cards. When attacking, if the enemy has no soldiers, you hit their library directly.
